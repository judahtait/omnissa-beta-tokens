Plan to finish the icon

  1. Pick a concept (my recommendation: D for family fit, or A if small-size clarity matters most) and a tile style.
  2. Check against the Omnissa brand guidelines and the official logo rules. Do not use Apple's logo or Apple marks, and do not reuse an existing Omnissa product mark, without brand approval. Status: guidelines not read; no official logo available.
  3. Draw the final artwork at 1024 px as vector, with text outlined (concept B). Graphite tile is generated the way HubControl's make-app-icons.swift does it.
  4. Liquid Glass version: compose layers (tile, glyph, specular) in Icon Composer (Xcode 26) for macOS 26. Keep a flat .icns for earlier systems. Needs full Xcode, which this Mac does not have.
  5. Export the 16 to 1024 px iconset to assets/AppIcon.icns. tools/make_app.sh already bundles it and sets CFBundleIconFile when the file exists.
  6. Reuse the mark in the guide header and the app header band.
