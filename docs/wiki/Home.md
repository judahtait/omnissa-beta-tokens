## Flow at a glance

1. Utility → Apple Business `.p7m` → org beta tokens  
2. UEM declarative **Software Update: Settings → Beta**  
3. Pilot Smart Group → publish → confirm on device  

Validated against Apple’s schema and the same pattern used by Jamf Blueprints, Fleet DDM, and Microsoft’s token script.
